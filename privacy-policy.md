# Privacy Policy for Sistani-GPT

**Effective date:** November 17, 2026

Sistani-GPT ("the app", "we", "our") is an Android app that provides information on Shia Islamic Fiqh knowledge using artificial intelligence. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number, address, or contact list.

The app processes the following:

- **Your questions and messages:** Text you type into the app is sent to a third-party AI service so that an answer can be generated.
- **Technical data:** Like most apps, basic technical information (such as device type, Android version, and crash reports) may be collected by Google Play services to keep the app working properly.

## 2. How We Use Information

Your messages are used only to generate answers inside the app. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

## 3. Third-Party Services

To generate answers, your messages are sent to the Google Gemini API, a service provided by Google. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Please do not enter sensitive personal information (passwords, bank details, national ID numbers) into the app.

## 4. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Data is shared only with the AI service described above, solely to provide the app's functionality.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

We do not maintain our own user database. Any conversation history shown in the app is stored locally on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. No method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Religious Information Disclaimer

Answers are generated by AI and may contain mistakes. They are for general information only and are not a religious ruling (fatwa). For important matters, please consult a qualified scholar or your Marja's official office.

## 8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal accounts or store your data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to delete. You can delete local data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
