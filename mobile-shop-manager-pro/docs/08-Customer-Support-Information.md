## Before you write

Most questions are answered in the guides included with your download:

1. **Installation Guide** — installing, where data is stored, updating
2. **Quick-Start Guide** — first sale in ten minutes
3. **User Manual** — every screen explained
4. **Licence Activation** — keys, offline use, moving computers
5. **Troubleshooting Guide** — common messages and fixes

## What to include in a support request

- Your Gumroad **order email** (never send your full licence key by email unless asked)
- App version (**Settings → About**)
- Windows version
- What you were doing, and the exact message shown — a screenshot helps
- For start-up problems: the `main.log` file described in the Troubleshooting Guide

## What support covers

- Installation, activation and licence transfer
- How to use any feature
- Problems restoring backups
- Bug reports

Support does not include accounting, tax or legal advice, or data entry on your behalf.

## Your data and privacy

Your shop data is stored only on your computer. We cannot see it and cannot recover it for you — **please keep regular backups**. If you send us a backup file for troubleshooting, it is used only for that purpose and then deleted.
