# Licence Activation

## Where is my licence key?

Your key is in the **receipt email from Gumroad** and on your Gumroad **Library** page for this product. It looks like this:

`1A2B3C4D-5E6F7A8B-9C0D1E2F-3A4B5C6D`

## Activating

1. Make sure the computer is connected to the internet (needed only for this step).
2. Open **Settings → Licence**.
3. Paste the key into **Activate software** and click **Activate**.
4. The status changes to **Licensed**.

## Trial

Without a key the app runs as a full-featured trial for **{{trialDays}} days**.

## Working offline

The software is designed for shops with unreliable internet.

- After activation, everyday work — inventory, sales, invoices, reports, backups — needs **no internet at all**.
- When a connection happens to be available, the app quietly re-checks the licence about once a week.
- It keeps working for up to **{{graceDays}} days** without a single successful check.
- After that it asks you to connect once and click **Verify now**.

**Your data is never locked.** If the trial ends, the offline allowance runs out, or a licence is refunded, the app switches to a restricted mode in which you can still **open, view, search, print, export and back up everything**. Only creating new records is paused until the licence is valid again.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| This licence key is not valid for Mobile Shop Manager Pro | The key was mistyped or belongs to another product | Copy and paste it from the receipt email |
| Could not reach the licence server | No internet connection, or a firewall is blocking it | Connect and try again; allow `api.gumroad.com` |
| …because the purchase was refunded | The order was refunded or charged back | Purchase again or contact support |
| …because the licence was disabled by the seller | The seller disabled the key | Contact support |
| …already been activated the maximum number of times | The key is in use on other computers | Remove it from an old computer or contact support |
| Verification needed | More than {{graceDays}} days since the last online check | Connect to the internet and click **Verify now** |

## Moving to a new computer

1. On the old computer: **Backup & Restore → Back up now**, and copy the backup file to a USB drive.
2. On the old computer: **Settings → Licence → Remove licence from this computer**.
3. Install the app on the new computer and activate with the same key.
4. **Backup & Restore → Restore from a backup file…** and choose your backup.

## Privacy

The only information sent over the internet is your licence key, to Gumroad's licence service, to confirm the purchase. No shop, customer or sales data ever leaves your computer.
