## Product subtitle / summary

## Product description

**Run your mobile shop from one clean screen.**

Mobile Shop Manager Pro is desktop software built specifically for mobile phone retailers. It keeps your phones, accessories, customers, credit, trade-ins, expenses and suppliers in one place and shows you, every day, what you sold and what you actually earned.

It works **offline**. Your data stays on **your computer**. You pay **once**.

**Made for how phone shops really work**

- Every phone is tracked by **IMEI**, and the same IMEI can never be entered twice.
- Sell a phone with accessories, a discount, a trade-in and part payment — on one screen, in under a minute.
- Customers who pay later go straight into the **Credit Book**, with receipts and statements.
- Trade-ins automatically become **used stock**, and you see the profit when you resell them.
- Print a professional **A4 invoice** or an **80 mm thermal receipt**, save it as PDF, or send the details by **WhatsApp**.

**Works in your country**

Choose your currency (or define your own), number format, date format, time zone and first day of the week. Set up VAT, GST or sales tax your way — inclusive or exclusive — or switch tax off. The interface is in English, with starter translations and right-to-left layout for Urdu and Arabic, plus Spanish, French, German and Hindi.

**Your numbers, done properly**

Profit is calculated per item and counted once, on the day of the sale. Credit payments reduce what the customer owes — they are never counted as extra profit. Tax you collect is kept out of profit. Expenses are deducted to show net profit.

**Safe by design**

Automatic daily backups, one-click manual backup, and a restore that validates the file and saves a safety copy first. Completed sales can be voided with a reason but never silently deleted. Optional password protection.

## Installation instructions

1. Download and unzip the file.
2. Run `MobileShopManagerPro-Setup-[version].exe` from the `1-Windows-Installer` folder (or use the portable version in `2-Portable-Version`).
3. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click **More info → Run anyway**.
4. Open the app, go to **Settings → Licence**, and paste the licence key from your Gumroad receipt.
5. Enter your shop details in **Settings** and start selling.

## FAQ

**Is this a subscription?**
No. You pay once and the software is yours to use. There are no monthly fees.

**Does it need the internet?**
Only once, to activate your licence. After that it works completely offline. When the internet is available it re-checks the licence quietly in the background.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your own computer, in a local database. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

**Will it work with my currency and tax?**
Yes. You choose the currency (or create a custom one), number and date formats, and you configure tax yourself — VAT, GST, sales tax or none; inclusive or exclusive.

**Does it guarantee tax compliance in my country?**
No. It calculates from the settings you enter. Tax rules vary; please check your obligations with an accountant.

[State your policy, e.g. "One licence covers one shop computer. Contact us to move it to a new computer."]

**Does it work on Mac or Linux?**
This package is for Windows 10 and 11.

**Can I print on a thermal receipt printer?**
Yes — any 80 mm receipt printer installed in Windows. A4/Letter printers work too.

**Does WhatsApp sending need a business API?**
No. It opens your normal WhatsApp with the message ready to send.

**What if my computer breaks?**
Restore your latest backup on the new computer. Keep backups on a USB drive or cloud folder — the app can back up automatically every day.

**Can I import my existing stock?**
Yes, from CSV files for mobiles, accessories, customers and suppliers. Templates are included in the app.

**What languages are supported?**
English is complete. Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, French, German and Hindi have starter translations of the main screens, with right-to-left layout for Urdu and Arabic.

**Windows shows a warning when I install. Is it safe?**
The "Windows protected your PC" message appears for software from smaller publishers. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Do you offer refunds?**
See the refund policy below.
